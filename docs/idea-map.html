What changed and why

01
One color for the whole logoThe logo stays all ink blue, exactly like your pen. A single color is simpler, cheaper to print and easier to recognize.
02
Even line weightPen lines vary in thickness. Evening them out looks professional and prints cleanly, while keeping your shapes.
03
A solid version for small sizesThin outlines blur when small. The solid version stays readable on phones, favicons and social avatars.
04
An app icon from your "m"Icons need one simple shape. Your own "m" is recognizable at 28px.
05
Two colors, used the same way every timeConsistency builds recognition. Blue is the brand. Coral is only for the most important buttons and verified badges.

New: name research

Picking the name

MindfulCrowded

Owned by meditation brands like Mindful.org, plus several software trademarks (Mindful AI, Mindful Software).

MindfullPossible, with care

Used by a nutrition app, a mental-health practice, flash cards and clothing. The one software trademark for it was abandoned in 2015, so software looks open. People may still think "wellness."

MindfolioOpen in our search

Mind + portfolio. Says exactly what you are: a portfolio that proves skill. Keeps your M mark. Nothing found under this name.

FullmindAvoid

Already used by a meditation app and a teacher-staffing company.

SkillfullRisky

Great pun for developers, but very close to Skillful AI, an AI builder marketplace.

Your callTop 2

Mindfull if you love your drawing as is. Mindfolio if you want a name that's easier to own and explains itself.

This is a quick web search, not a legal check. Before buying a domain or printing anything, run a free search at the USPTO trademark site or ask a lawyer.
